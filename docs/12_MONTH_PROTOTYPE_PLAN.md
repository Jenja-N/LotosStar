# Lotos Star — 12-Month Prototype Plan

## Objective

Build and publicly demonstrate **Lotos Star Prototype 01**: a functioning transformable planetarium environment that proves the core thesis of the project.

> A single physical space can operate as a school, laboratory, theatre, observatory, immersive cinema, cultural archive, and contemplative environment.

The 12-month objective is **not** to launch a global network. It is to produce one credible, measurable, documented prototype and the first body of original content.

---

## Success definition

At the end of 12 months, an external observer should be able to answer “yes” to all of the following:

1. Does a real prototype exist?
2. Can real people enter it and experience the concept?
3. Does the dome support more than astronomy?
4. Is there original educational and cultural content?
5. Is there a documented technical architecture?
6. Is there evidence that audiences learn, engage, or respond differently inside this format?
7. Could another serious team reproduce or adapt the concept from the documentation?
8. Is there enough evidence to justify a larger permanent hub?

---

## Workstreams

### 1. Physical prototype

Deliver:

- dome geometry and structural specification;
- projection surface solution;
- entrance / circulation concept;
- seating / floor transformation concept;
- climate and ventilation solution appropriate to Thailand;
- acoustic treatment concept;
- electrical and safety plan;
- bill of materials;
- fabrication and assembly documentation.

The prototype may begin as a temporary or semi-permanent structure. The scientific goal is to prove the **institutional format**, not to maximize architectural prestige.

### 2. Projection, audio, and control stack

Deliver:

- fulldome projection pipeline;
- projection calibration method;
- spatial or multi-channel audio;
- playback/control software;
- presenter/operator interface;
- interactive input experiments where technically justified;
- documentation of hardware and software dependencies.

Design principle:

> Prefer a reproducible system over an unnecessarily expensive proprietary stack.

### 3. KosmoLingua v1

Build the first operational version of the interdisciplinary educational framework.

Each program should connect at least three domains.

Example:

**Saturn**
- astronomy;
- physics;
- visual culture;
- mythology/history;
- navigation;
- sound/art;
- philosophical questions about scale and time.

KosmoLingua should not become an abstract philosophy document. It must be demonstrated through actual programs.

### 4. Original immersive programs

Target: **3–5 prototype programs**.

Minimum set:

#### A. Science / Astronomy
A rigorous fulldome experience based on modern astronomy.

#### B. Culture / Human Story
A documentary or cultural program linking sky, cosmology, and lived human traditions.

Candidate: **Under One Sky**.

#### C. Attention / Consciousness
A carefully framed contemplative experience combining sensory design with evidence-aware discussion of attention and perception.

#### D. Art / Performance
An audiovisual or generative work using the dome as an artistic medium.

Optional fifth program:
Ecology / systems thinking / Earth observation.

### 5. Audience pilots

Run structured pilots with several audience groups:

- school-age learners;
- university students or educators;
- general adult audiences;
- artists / creators;
- mixed international audiences.

Capture:

- attendance;
- completion;
- qualitative reactions;
- interviews;
- teacher observations;
- simple pre/post knowledge checks where appropriate;
- willingness to return or recommend;
- what audiences remember after a delay.

This is not intended to overclaim educational efficacy from a small pilot. The goal is to collect early evidence and identify what works.

### 6. Documentation

Publish:

- system architecture;
- dome design assumptions;
- bill of materials;
- software architecture;
- content production pipeline;
- KosmoLingua framework;
- operational lessons;
- pilot methodology;
- failures and revisions;
- replication constraints.

The documentation should be useful enough that a future partner can distinguish what is proven from what remains aspirational.

### 7. Public proof

By the end of the year, produce:

- short documentary / project film;
- high-quality photography;
- technical walkthrough;
- public website/repository;
- concise impact report;
- prototype demonstration event.

## Milestones

### Months 1–2 — Definition

- freeze Prototype 01 scope;
- complete site requirements;
- select dome format;
- define projection/audio architecture;
- define first four content programs;
- establish budget;
- identify fabrication and scientific/cultural collaborators.

**Exit criterion:** buildable design + credible budget + production calendar.

### Months 3–4 — Engineering

- dome CAD / structural work;
- projection tests;
- audio tests;
- software control prototype;
- first content storyboards;
- pilot measurement design.

**Exit criterion:** technical stack proven at reduced scale.

### Months 5–6 — Build

- fabricate or procure structure;
- install projection/audio;
- integrate control system;
- conduct calibration;
- test environmental and operational constraints.

**Exit criterion:** operational dome prototype.

### Months 7–8 — Content

- complete first astronomy program;
- complete first cultural/storytelling program;
- complete first attention/contemplative program;
- complete first art/performance program.

**Exit criterion:** minimum viable program library.

### Months 9–10 — Pilots

- run school and public sessions;
- collect structured feedback;
- revise programs;
- revise operator workflow;
- document failure modes.

**Exit criterion:** repeatable audience sessions.

### Months 11–12 — Proof and publication

- public demonstration;
- documentary and photography;
- publish technical and educational documentation;
- write Prototype 01 report;
- define Prototype 02 / permanent hub requirements.

**Exit criterion:** independent reviewer can verify that Lotos Star is a working institution prototype, not merely a concept.

---

## Budget logic

Exact figures should be produced only after site and dome decisions are frozen.

Budget categories:

1. founder/applicant compensation;
2. structure and fabrication;
3. projection;
4. audio;
5. computing/control;
6. content production;
7. scientific/cultural consultants;
8. local transport and fieldwork;
9. insurance/safety/permits;
10. pilot operations;
11. documentation;
12. contingency.

Funding should prioritize **proof-generating expenditure**.

Avoid early spending on:

- permanent luxury architecture;
- large hospitality components;
- branding agencies;
- speculative real estate;
- oversized teams;
- features that do not strengthen the prototype thesis.

---

## Decision rule

Every feature should answer:

> Does this help prove that Lotos Star is a new, reproducible cultural and educational institution?

If not, defer it.

---

## End state

At month 12, Lotos Star should be able to move from:

**“Here is the idea.”**

to:

**“Here is the place. Here are the people using it. Here is what we learned. Here is how it works. Here is what the next version requires.”**
