# NatGeo Human Thread — submission checklist

## 1. Funding Portal

- Create or log into National Geographic Society Funding Portal.
- Open **RFP: The Human Thread**.
- Confirm deadline and exact submission time.
- Confirm whether Level I / Level II options appear.
- Confirm whether applicant nationality/residency triggers any additional eligibility questions.

## 2. Eligibility

Confirm:

- age 18+;
- application in English;
- applicant listed as project leader / narrative lead;
- primary discipline = Storytelling;
- primary focus area = Human Histories and Cultures;
- no other active application as project leader;
- previous NGS grants, if any, fully closed;
- project start date at least six months after deadline.

## 3. Application fields

Copy/adapt text from [APPLICATION_DRAFT.md](APPLICATION_DRAFT.md).

Do not paste blindly if portal character limits differ.

Priority fields:

- title;
- summary;
- significance;
- objectives;
- methodology/story approach;
- qualifications;
- local connection;
- outputs;
- audiences;
- impact;
- ethics;
- team;
- media commitments.

## 4. Funding level

Preferred: **Level II / $100,000**, if the live RFP permits and applicant is eligible.

Fallback: **Level I / $20,000**.

Never submit a $100k scope with a $20k budget.

## 5. Budget

For every requested line:

- exact amount;
- reason;
- quantity × rate;
- price reference where possible;
- only costs directly required for Under One Sky.

Remove all Lotos Star construction costs.

## 6. Portfolio

- strongest documentary work;
- human/cultural emphasis;
- concise captions;
- no filler;
- no weak tourist photography.

## 7. Team

Only list people who have agreed to participate.

Before submission, ideally confirm:

- Thailand-based translator/fixer/cultural producer;
- Buddhist studies/cultural adviser;
- astronomy adviser;
- film/post-production support if named.

## 8. Ethics

Review [ETHICS_AND_RISK.md](ETHICS_AND_RISK.md).

Particularly verify:

- minors;
- migrants;
- Myanmar-connected participants;
- consent;
- participant identification;
- political/security risk.

## 9. Citizenship / compliance

Applicant is a Russian citizen based in Thailand.

Before submission or award acceptance:

- answer nationality/residency questions exactly;
- do not conceal citizenship;
- confirm payment route;
- verify any OFAC/sanctions review;
- verify bank acceptance;
- verify Thai tax/legal implications.

## 10. Final editorial review

Remove:

- buzzwords;
- Lotos Star fundraising language;
- “science proves Buddhism” language;
- exaggerated claims;
- unsupported impact figures;
- unnecessary technical details.

Strengthen:

- people;
- access;
- visual scenes;
- cultural specificity;
- why this applicant;
- why now;
- why this story matters beyond one religion.

## 11. Submit

- export/save a complete copy of every answer;
- save final budget;
- save upload files;
- screenshot/record submission confirmation;
- record application ID;
- retain exact submitted version in this repository privately or in a non-public location if it contains sensitive personal data.

## Final test

A reviewer should understand the proposal in 30 seconds:

> **A photographer, educator, writer, and former Buddhist monk living in Thailand will document how Buddhist communities reinterpret inherited cosmologies while encountering modern astronomy — through photography, oral history, film, and immersive storytelling.**
