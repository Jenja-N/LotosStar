# National Geographic — The Human Thread application package

## Project

**Under One Sky — Buddhism, cosmology, astronomy, and the human search for meaning in Myanmar and Thailand**

This folder contains a working submission package for the National Geographic Society 2026 RFP **The Human Thread**.

## Verified current facts

- Deadline: **1 December 2026**
- Application language: **English**
- Primary discipline: **Storytelling**
- Primary focus area: **Human Histories and Cultures**
- Project leader must be the person responsible for creating and/or narratively weaving the project
- Applications are submitted through the **National Geographic Society Funding Portal**
- General proposals by email are not accepted
- Project start should be at least **six months after the submission deadline**
- National Geographic's February 2026 budget guidance allows:
  - applicant/team compensation for eligible freelancers/non-salaried applicants;
  - assistant/consultant compensation;
  - airfare;
  - equipment and supplies;
  - food;
  - lodging;
  - measurement/evaluation;
  - local transportation;
  - safety/well-being/inclusion resources;
  - vehicle rental/maintenance;
  - institutional overhead up to 15%, where applicable.
- The same budget guidance says freelance storytellers should include their own day rate because publishing partners may publish grant-funded work fee-free.

## Funding strategy

Secondary university funding trackers for this exact RFP report:

- **Level I:** up to USD 20,000
- **Level II:** up to USD 100,000

The primary strategy is **Level II / USD 100,000**, because the applicant has a long record across photography, writing, education, technology, and Southeast Asian Buddhist contexts.

A Level I fallback budget is included in case the Funding Portal determines that Level II is not available or appropriate.

**The final amount and level must be verified inside the live Funding Portal before submission.**

## Strategic boundary

National Geographic is **not** being asked to finance Lotos Star as an institution.

The grant-facing object is **Under One Sky**, a standalone cultural storytelling project.

Lotos Star benefits later because the resulting documentary/photographic/immersive work can become a flagship cultural program, subject to the grant's publication and licensing terms.
