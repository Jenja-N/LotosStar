# Line-item reasons

## Applicant / Team Member Compensation — $33,000

Compensation for the project leader's time as principal storyteller, photographer, interviewer, writer, field producer, editor, and immersive-story director.

The work spans approximately 18 months and includes research, access-building, field production, photography, interviews, writing, editing supervision, fact-checking, participant follow-up, and final delivery.

The rate should be expressed in the portal as a defensible freelance day rate multiplied by a realistic number of project days.

National Geographic's February 2026 guidance explicitly permits compensation for eligible freelancers/non-salaried applicants and advises freelance storytellers to include their day rate because publishing partners may receive fee-free publication rights to grant-funded work.

## Assistant / Consultant Compensation — $16,000

For locally contracted:

- translator/fixer/cultural producer;
- transcription/translation support;
- astronomy consultant;
- Buddhist studies/cultural consultant;
- documentary editor or post-production specialist;
- immersive/fulldome technical support.

Local cultural labor must be compensated rather than treated as unpaid access.

## Airfare — $6,000

Regional travel required for field production, collaborator meetings, and selected story locations.

The budget assumes economy travel and should be revised with actual route quotes before submission.

The project can be reduced to Thailand-based work if direct Myanmar travel is not safe or feasible.

## Equipment and Supplies — $10,000

Only project-essential equipment.

Potential items:

- audio recorder and microphones;
- compact documentary video support;
- storage and backup media;
- batteries/power;
- field lighting where necessary;
- telescope/astronomy observation equipment used directly in documented encounters;
- calibration / immersive-media production supplies.

Existing owned photography equipment should be used where suitable rather than replaced without justification.

Every purchased item should receive a model/price reference in the portal where possible.

## Food — $4,500

Field-day meals for project leader and eligible team members during travel/production periods.

Use real local per-day estimates in the final portal budget.

## Lodging — $7,000

Modest field lodging during production outside the applicant's normal residence.

The final justification should include actual estimated nightly rates and number of nights.

## Other Transportation — $6,000

Local ground transport including:

- taxis;
- buses;
- rail;
- hired local transport;
- ferries where required;
- transport of production equipment.

Avoid vehicle rental unless it is demonstrably more efficient or necessary.

## Safety / well-being / inclusion resources — $3,000

Possible costs:

- travel/medical insurance;
- communications;
- safety planning;
- accessibility support;
- participant-protection measures;
- secure transport where necessary;
- protective cases or weather protection for field equipment.

## Measurement and Evaluation — $2,500

For:

- participant feedback;
- audience questionnaires at selected screenings;
- translation of feedback;
- evaluator/consultant review where useful;
- simple educational/audience comprehension assessment.

The purpose is responsible evaluation, not inflated impact claims.

## Miscellaneous / post-production services — $12,000

For directly required production services that do not fit better elsewhere, potentially including:

- color grading;
- sound mix;
- subtitles;
- transcription;
- mastering;
- digital-story implementation;
- fulldome conversion/rendering;
- archive/media management;
- community screening costs.

Each item should be placed in a more specific portal category if one is available.
