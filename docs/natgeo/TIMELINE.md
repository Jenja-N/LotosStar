# Under One Sky — 18-month timeline

## Proposed dates

**15 June 2027 – 15 December 2028**

## Months 1–3 — Research, access, ethics

- confirm communities and story leads;
- formalize translators/fixers/cultural collaborators;
- preliminary interviews;
- consent workflow;
- scientific and cultural research;
- astronomy consultant review;
- field safety review;
- travel feasibility assessment;
- production schedule.

### Milestone

At least three viable character/story threads confirmed.

## Months 4–8 — Primary field production

- documentary photography;
- observational video;
- oral histories;
- field sound;
- night-sky encounters;
- monastery/school/community contexts;
- visual documentation of cosmological art, architecture, and teaching materials.

### Milestone

Core photographic and interview archive complete.

## Months 9–11 — Secondary production and gap filling

- follow-up interviews;
- intergenerational comparison;
- additional astronomy encounters;
- missing visual sequences;
- verification with cultural/scientific advisers.

### Milestone

No major narrative gap remains dependent on speculative access.

## Months 12–14 — Edit and narrative assembly

- photo edit;
- transcription and translation;
- film edit;
- long-form writing;
- fact-checking;
- participant safety review;
- sound edit.

### Milestone

Complete first cuts/drafts of all primary story forms.

## Months 15–16 — Digital and immersive adaptation

- digital story;
- fulldome/immersive sequence;
- subtitles;
- educational discussion guide;
- accessibility review.

### Milestone

Publication-ready cross-media package.

## Month 17 — Community feedback and final revisions

- selected screenings/presentations;
- participant representation feedback;
- educator feedback;
- limited audience comprehension evaluation;
- corrections and final edit.

## Month 18 — Delivery

- final assets;
- final report;
- metadata;
- required grant media;
- archive organization;
- public/community delivery according to publication terms.

## Schedule guardrail

If travel inside Myanmar is not safe or feasible, the timeline does not stop.

Myanmar-connected stories can be documented through communities in Thailand and remote/archival approaches where ethically appropriate.
