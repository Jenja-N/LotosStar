# NatGeo Human Thread — source ledger

Checked: **30 October 2026**

This file records the sources used to prepare the application package. Re-check the live Funding Portal immediately before submission.

## Primary sources

### National Geographic Society — Grants & Investments
https://www.nationalgeographic.org/explore/grants-investments

Current official page confirms:

- The Human Thread is an active RFP.
- Deadline: 1 December 2026.
- The RFP seeks storytelling projects illuminating the human experience.
- Applications must go through the National Geographic Funding Portal.
- General proposals by email/mail are not accepted.
- Applicants must be 18+.
- Project start should be at least six months after the submission deadline.
- Applicant should have strong connection to local context.
- One project-leader proposal may be active at a time.
- Project leader should write the application.
- National Geographic complies with OFAC sanctions and other applicable law.

### National Geographic Society — Budget Guidelines, updated February 2026
https://images.nationalgeographic.org/image/upload/v1784570721/NGS%20Main%20Site/Grants%20and%20Investments/Budget_Guidelines.pdf

Confirms:

- budget entered in USD;
- requested budget should include only the amount requested from NGS;
- every funded line needs a reason/description;
- eligible categories include travel, lodging, food, equipment/supplies, safety resources, compensation, and evaluation;
- applicant/team compensation is allowed for eligible freelancers/non-salaried applicants;
- freelance storytellers should incorporate day rate because publishing partners may have fee-free publication rights;
- institutional overhead may be up to 15%;
- project year count follows project start/end dates.

### National Geographic Funding Portal
https://funding.nationalgeographic.org/

Live portal controls the final application fields, eligibility questions, funding level, uploads, and submission.

## Current RFP-specific secondary sources

These are not substitutes for the live portal but provide useful RFP-specific details.

### University of Florida College of Journalism and Communications — 23 October 2026
https://www.jou.ufl.edu/2026/10/23/research-funding-opportunities-october-23-2026/

Reports for The Human Thread:

- Human Histories and Cultures;
- projects documenting origins, evolutions, and current expressions of global cultures and religions;
- goal of bridging ancient legacies and contemporary realities;
- Level I up to USD 20,000;
- Level II up to USD 100,000;
- indirect costs limited to 15%.

### University of Virginia Corporate and Foundation Relations
https://cfr.virginia.edu/faculty-staff/funding-opportunities/human-thread-program

Reports:

- deadline 1 December 2026;
- award amount up to USD 100,000;
- storytelling focus.

### California for the Arts
https://www.caforthearts.org/jobs/national-geographic-society-the-human-thread-grant-2026

Reports RFP-specific eligibility including:

- primary discipline Storytelling;
- primary focus area Human Histories and Cultures.
